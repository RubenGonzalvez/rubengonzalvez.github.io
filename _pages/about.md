I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the Complutense University of Madrid. My research lies at the intersection of labor economics, empirical macroeconomics, and applied microeconometrics. I use administrative data and quasi-experimental designs to study how policy shocks such as minimum wage hikes, monetary policy, and fiscal redistribution affect firms, workers, and households.
